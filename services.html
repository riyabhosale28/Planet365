How to Reduce?

1.Reduce Waste

--Avoid Single-Use items.
--Opt for Minimal Packaging.
--Compost Organic Waste.

2.Conserve Resources

-Save Energy:Turn off lights and electronics when not in use,and switch to energy-efficient appliances.
-Save Water:Fix leaks,use water-saving fixtures,and turn off taps when not needed.
-Buy Only What you need:Avoid impulse purchases to reduce waste and clutter.

3.Limit paper use

-Go Digital:Use electronic documents,e-tickits,and online banking instead of printing.
-Reuse paper:Write on both sides or repurpose it for notes or crafts.

4.Encourage Others

--Spread awareness about reducing waste and conserving resouces.
--Participate in community programs promoting sustainability.

How to Reuse?

--To reuse means to use items multiple times instead of discarding them after a single use.

1.Reuse everyday items

--Use cloth or sturdy bags for shopping instead of plastic bags.
--Carry a reusable water bottle to avoid disposals.

2.Repurpose old items

--Turn old clothes into cleaning rags,tote bags,or donate them if they are in good condition.
--Refurbish or repaint old furniture instead of throwing it away.

3.Get Creative

--Transfer old materials into new items.

How to Recycle?

1.Understand what can be recycled

--Paper and cardboard,plastics,Glass,Metals,Electronics

2.Sort materials

--Seperate recyclables by type(paper,plastic,metal,glass).
Use clearly labeled bins for easier sorting at home or in the office

3.Clean and prepare Materials

--Reside out containers to remove food residue.
flatten boxes to save space.

4.Recycle special items

some items require special handling:

-Batteries and Electronics:take to e-waste recycling centers.
-Plastic Bags:Return to drop-off bins at supermarkets or retailers.
-Textiles:Donate usable clothings or take worn-out items to textile recycling programs.
